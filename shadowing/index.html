How to shadow
  • Listen once without reading. Turn on Hide the words if you want a real challenge.
  • Say it with the speaker, not after. Copy the rhythm and the joins, not each word.
  • Hit the bold words hard. Let the light words go quick and small.
  • Follow the arrows. Each chunk has one word where your voice moves: down when you're finished or sure, up when you're asking or not done yet, down then up for "yes, but...".
  • Record, then play Speaker, then me. If your waveform is much longer, you're stressing too many words.
  • Keys: Space listen, R record or stop, arrow keys for the next line.
