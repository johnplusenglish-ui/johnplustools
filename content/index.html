The format these were written against

Read from the existing reels on the account: GTA 5 explained, why people hate noobs, stuck at 300 views, pretending to be asleep, why mums never answer, Ali Dia the fake footballer, the 500 million dollar art heist, the World Cup found by a dog, the Alcatraz escape, the D.B. Cooper mystery.

  • Length: 30 to 60 seconds, vertical, one voice, no presenter on camera.
  • Opening line is the whole story compressed into one sentence with the ending withheld.
  • Thumbnail is a bold top title plus a red banner line at the bottom holding the twist.
  • Everything is staged with Roblox avatars and sets, however serious the subject.
  • Caption is two or three short lines, then a direct question to the viewer, then hashtags.
  • Two pillars: things that really happened, and things everybody quietly wonders about.

Each idea below carries a title, a banner line, a hook, the story, the twist, four shot beats and a caption question, so it can go straight into a video build.
