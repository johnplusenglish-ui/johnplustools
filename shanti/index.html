Rome, Italy · Live music agency · Researched 18 Nov 2026

Shanti and Son(g)s Entertainment

A Roman live-music company that supplies the whole sound of a luxury event, from a string quartet under a pergola at four o'clock to a six-voice show band at midnight. It is built around the singer Valentina Shanti and trades as Shanti&Songs S.R.L. from an address in the north of Rome.

The offer

What they actually do

They describe themselves not as a band but as an agency: a pool of musicians and performers who are assembled into whatever shape the event needs. Their own site has four doors into the business.

01

Weddings

The core trade. An acoustic duo for the ceremony, a string quartet for the quiet interludes, a live band for the reception and a DJ for the dance floor, with song choices and the performers' clothing agreed in advance.

02

Corporate

Two ends of a range: discreet background music for formal meetings, and high-energy shows for product launches and team-building days. Their client wall is dominated by banks, pharma and tech.

03

Parties

Private celebrations and birthdays. Live band or DJ, custom shows, and a repertoire built to keep a floor full rather than to be admired from a chair.

04

Worldwide

Destination work. Based in Italy, available abroad, and willing to go somewhere they have never played. A 2026 couple booked them for Tivat in Montenegro, which was a first for the band.

Shanti&Songs Entertainment is the key to luxury musical experiences.

Their own opening line, used across the website, the Showgroup listing and the agency directories

Configurations

The line-ups they sell

This is the most useful thing in their archive. The YouTube channel is organised as a catalogue of formats rather than a highlights reel, so you can see exactly what a client is choosing between. Sizes run from one voice and a guitar up to a six-piece vocal show.

Vocal Sextet

Their flagship. Six voices, sold in two versions: a ceremony set and a party show. The ceremony film has about 7,000 views, the most-watched thing they have posted.

String Quartet

Two violins, viola and cello for ceremonies and drinks receptions. Reviewers mention it repeatedly for the aisle and the aperitivo.

Live Band

The full reception and party set. This is the configuration behind most of the Villa Miani and Villa Aurelia footage.

Laser Harp

The showpiece. A harp played on beams of light, pitched as the shape of a classical instrument with the sound and visual punch of an electronic one.

Sax and Electronic Pads

A saxophonist over electronic backing. Sold as an event show rather than background music, and the format that turns up in most of their recent Instagram clips.

Vale Shanti Trio and 4TeT

Valentina's own small groups, three and four pieces, used for dinner sets and for the more musically serious bookings.

To Be Duo

A named two-piece used for real weddings. The smallest standard product, and the one that typically covers a ceremony.

Voice and guitar

Offered with a female or a male singer, and in an extended version with a violin added. The quiet default for ceremonies and early evening.

Voice and piano

Also offered male or female, and as a trio with saxophone. The dinner option.

Spring Rolls

A named trio of voice, violin and bass. One of several small projects that carry their own branding rather than a generic label.

Swing Female Project

A jazz and swing set fronted by a female vocalist, leaning on the Ella Fitzgerald and Etta James end of their influences.

Loop station set

Male voice, guitar and a loop station. One performer building a full arrangement live, which is cheap to stage and holds a room.

Brass

A horn section, listed among their projects. Used for arrivals and for lifting a party set.

DJ set

Either on its own or handed the floor after the band. Several reviews describe a band-then-DJ handover running to the end of the night.

Circus Party

A themed show with performers rather than only musicians, filmed as a showreel about five years ago. The clearest sign that they sell staging and not just sound.

Amuser

Listed as a project on their own navigation. No public description survives, so treat this one as unconfirmed.

How it fits together

A wedding day, as they stage it

Their wedding page and their reviews agree on the same four-part shape. A client is really buying a sequence, not a band.

1

Ceremony

Acoustic duo, or the string quartet for the aisle and the signing.

2

Aperitivo

Strings or solo saxophone over the drinks reception and the photographs.

3

Dinner

Vocalists and a small group, including a cued entrance song for the couple.

4

Party

Full live band, then the DJ takes the floor through to the end of the night.

Two details they push hard in their own copy: the setlist is rewritten per client, and the performers' clothing is treated as part of the product rather than an afterthought.

Who they are

The people

Founder and lead vocalist

Valentina Shanti

Stage name of Valentina Gatti, an Italian singer, performer and vocal coach based in Rome. She trained in popular and modern music at the Saint Louis College of Music in Rome, and the company is built around her voice and her contacts.

  • Fronted the band White Gold, which played the Saint Louis Jammin' festival in Rome in September 2015 alongside around forty young Italian artists.
  • Sat on the hundred-strong human wall of judges on the Canale 5 talent show All Together Now, in both the first series (May to June 2019) and the second (December 2019 to January 2020), billed in the Mediaset cast list as Valentina Gatti, alias Valentina Shanti.
  • Has taught singing at Mob Studios in Rome since at least 2017.
  • Described in the trade directories as having more than a decade in event entertainment, covering weddings, corporate parties, birthdays, banquets and anniversaries.
  • Her own small groups, Vale Shanti Trio and Vale Shanti 4TeT, still run inside the company as named products.
Office and planning

Paolo

Named repeatedly in client reviews as the person who answers emails, runs the planning calls and builds the running order. No surname is published. In practice he appears to be the coordinator between the couple, their planner and whichever musicians are booked.

The pool

No full roster is published anywhere. What the reviews and the video catalogue confirm is a rotating pool that includes violinists, a violist and a cellist, saxophonists, guitarists, pianists, a bassist, a brass section, DJs, additional male and female vocalists and, for the themed shows, non-musical performers. The registry filing records three employees for 2026, which fits a tiny permanent core hiring freelancers per event.

Also credited

LZM Communication

The agency credited in the footer of every page of shantiandsongs.com, with a 2024 copyright line. The current site is their work, which dates the present brand presentation to 2024.

How it got here

History

There is no published founding story, so this timeline is assembled from dated public traces: festival listings, broadcast cast lists, video upload dates, the registry filing and the reviews.

  1. 2015 White Gold and the Saint Louis scene

    Valentina Shanti is listed as the vocalist of White Gold at the Jammin' festival run by the Saint Louis College of Music in Rome, 12 September 2015, playing with Thomas Rocca on keys, Lucio Mastroianni on guitar, Stefano Zandomeneghi on bass and Luca Avvantaggiato on drums. The Rome conservatoire circuit is where the musician pool comes from.

  2. 2017 Teaching

    She appears on the Mob Studios roster in Rome as a singing teacher, a post dated September 2017.

  3. 2019 to 2020 National television

    Two consecutive series on the judging wall of All Together Now on Canale 5. A hundred names, so not stardom, but national visibility at exactly the point the event business was being built.

  4. Around 2021 The catalogue is filmed

    The oldest surviving videos on the Shanti&Songs channel are showreels rather than real events: vocal sextet, string quartet, laser harp, circus party, sax and electronic pads. This is a company deliberately building a product menu to sell from.

  5. 2022 to 2023 Real weddings in Roman villas

    The uploads switch from showreels to named venues: Villa Astor, Villa Aurelia, Villa Dino, Villa Miani, and La Lanterna in Rome. The duo and trio formats appear alongside the full band.

  6. 2024 The company and the current brand

    Shanti&Songs S.R.L. is on the Rome register at Via Gaspara Stampa 63 with a share capital of 10,000 euro, trading under the party and ceremony organisation code. Turnover for 2024 is recorded at 233,235 euro with a profit of 23,006 euro. The present website goes up, credited to LZM Communication.

  7. 2025 Growth

    Declared turnover rises to 316,630 euro, about 36 per cent up on the previous year. A Florence wedding at Villa Corsini in July draws one of the warmest reviews on file.

  8. 2026 Abroad, and up the rankings

    Bookings at the Odescalchi Castle in Bracciano in March, Villa Aurelia in May, Montenegro in June and July, Abbazia San Giusto near Viterbo in June. They place sixth on one directory's list of the twenty-three best wedding bands in Rome, and fifteenth for Italy as a whole. A film from Palazzo Shedir, the Leading Hotels of the World property inside Palazzo Borghese, goes up in the summer.

What the research could not settle

  • No founding date. No incorporation date is published for the S.R.L. The VAT number and REA both point to a recent registration, probably 2022 or 2023, but that is inference from number ranges, not a sourced fact. The performing act clearly predates the company by several years.
  • No named roster. Beyond Valentina and a first-name reference to Paolo, no musician is publicly named by the company.
  • Project pages have been removed. Search engines still hold entries for individual project pages such as the laser harp, but those URLs now return a 404. The current site is a single page with anchors, so some of the detail quoted in directories is no longer live on their own domain.
  • La Librata appears on their venue wall with no matching public venue record.
  • The team copy on their own site is broken. The About section switches between they, her and she in a single paragraph and still talks only about weddings, which reads as leftover text from an earlier single-artist profile that was never rewritten for the agency.
  • TikTok could not be read. The account requires a login to view, so nothing from it is included here.
