Tune check

Listen to the speaker, say the line, and see how close your tune is. Nothing you record leaves your phone.

The blue words are the ones the speaker's voice moves on.

SpeakerYouEach voice is drawn around its own middle, so a deep voice is fine.
How this check works
  • Your phone measures how high your voice goes up and down, many times a second.
  • It lines your recording up with the speaker's by the sounds you make, so each of your words sits under the speaker's word.
  • Then it compares the two tunes, one chunk at a time. Both voices are measured against their own middle, so it does not matter if your voice is higher or lower.
  • It checks the tune, not your sounds. It cannot tell you if a word was wrong.
Numbers for testing

For John. The distance is how far apart the two tunes are, in semitones (a piano key is one semitone). Under the first number is Same tune, up to the second is Nearly, above is Try again.
